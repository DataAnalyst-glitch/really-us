If you already paid, clicked, or shared details

Fictional demo content. Northline Power is a fictional company created for a hackathon demonstration. All contact details are demonstration details.

Summary

If you already paid, clicked a link, installed an app, or shared details, act now and contact a person. Northline cannot promise that money can be recovered.

What to do now, in order

  • 1. Stop replying to the sender and do not send more money.
  • 2. Contact your bank or card provider using the number on your card or official website.
  • 3. If you installed an app or allowed remote access, disconnect from the internet and remove it.
  • 4. Change any password you shared.
  • 5. Report the incident to your country's official fraud reporting service.
  • 6. Ask this assistant for the Northline fraud desk.

What the fraud desk does

The fraud desk records the case, confirms what Northline did or did not send, and gives you a reference for your report.

What Northline cannot do

Northline cannot reverse a payment made outside Northline, and cannot promise recovery of lost money.
