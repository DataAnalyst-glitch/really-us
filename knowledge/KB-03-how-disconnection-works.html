How disconnection really works at Northline Power

Fictional demo content. Northline Power is a fictional company created for a hackathon demonstration. All contact details are demonstration details.

Summary

Northline Power never disconnects service on the same day as a first warning. A written notice comes at least 15 days before any disconnection.

The process

  • 1. A bill becomes overdue.
  • 2. Northline sends a reminder through an official channel.
  • 3. Northline sends a written disconnection notice with a Proof Code at least 15 days before the date.
  • 4. You can pay or ask for help through an official channel before that date.

Messages that say tonight

A message saying power will be cut today or tonight does not match how a first warning works. Do not call the number in the message yet. Paste the message here so it can be checked.

What this assistant can and cannot confirm

  • From a general question, this assistant cannot confirm the status of your account or promise that your service will not be disconnected.
  • An earlier written notice may already exist on an account. To check, paste the message or give the Proof Code.
