Northline Power billing basics

Fictional demo content. Northline Power is a fictional company created for a hackathon demonstration. All contact details are demonstration details.

Summary

A higher bill usually comes from higher use, a longer billing period, an estimated reading, or a tariff change. This assistant explains common reasons and cannot see a live bill unless a demo account is checked.

Common reasons a bill changes

  • Higher electricity use.
  • A longer billing period.
  • An earlier estimated reading being corrected by an actual reading.
  • A change in tariff.

How to pay through official channels

Pay only through northline.example or another official channel. Northline never asks you to pay through a person's account or by gift card.

Refunds and disputes

This assistant cannot approve refunds, credits, or bill changes. It can connect you with a person who can review your bill.
